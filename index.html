About the Firm

Shirin Shoaei Law Office

Legal questions in Iran require precise knowledge of the Iranian Civil and Family Code as well as local court practice. Many Iranians living abroad face huge hurdles when property issues or family matters in their home country need to be settled.

Our office is led by Shirin Shoaei, an attorney licensed by the Iranian Judiciary (member of the Judiciary's Bar Association - مرکز وکلا). Our philosophy is to give you security through direct representation on the ground, create transparency about the state of your proceedings, and defend your rights in Iran in the best possible way.
